Gmail connections

Open Settings, then Add Gmail account. Each connected account has its own Disconnect control. Select your sender before confirming an email. Reply tracking checks the original Gmail conversation; an unrelated new conversation may not appear as a reply.

Your records

Projects are saved on your device. Export project records before changing devices or deleting the app. Account sign-in does not automatically move local projects between devices.

Delete your account

In Settings, choose Delete account. For assistance, email us from your account's address. We may need to verify ownership. Deleting First Five does not withdraw messages already sent through Gmail.
