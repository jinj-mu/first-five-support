First Five Privacy

Updated October 27, 2026. First Five is operated by Ouro LLC.

Your account and projects

We store your account email, a salted password hash, and session records to provide access to your account. Project setup, prospect details, drafts, outreach notes and downloaded replies are stored in your First Five account and cached on your device so they can sync when you sign in on another device. These records are encrypted in our database. They are also processed by our service when needed to research people, draft messages, send email, or check replies.

Google and Gmail

Google verification and a Gmail connection are required to activate your First Five account. We request your email identity and permission to send messages and read Gmail. Additional Gmail accounts can be connected in Settings. Sending happens when you review and confirm an email. Reply checks read the Gmail conversations associated with your tracked outreach; we do not use Gmail messages to train AI models or for advertising. If you separately enable automatic reply drafts, the relevant incoming reply text and your previous outreach message are sent to OpenAI to prepare a response. Drafts require your approval before sending. You can turn automatic response drafts off in Outreach. Google credentials are encrypted in our database and are never included in the mobile application.

Voice project intake

If you choose voice intake, First Five records only after you tap Start recording and allow microphone access. Recording is limited to three minutes and stops when the app leaves the foreground. Before uploading a recording, we ask permission to send it to OpenAI for transcription. You can edit the transcript before requesting AI organization into project fields. Our service processes audio in memory without saving the audio to its database. The temporary device recording is removed after successful transcription or when you leave intake; interrupted sessions may leave a temporary cache file until system cleanup. Provider retention policies still apply. You may type or fill setup manually instead.

AI processing

When you request research or drafting, your supplied setup, prospect facts and relevant exclusions are sent to OpenAI. This can include personal information you put into those fields. Requests use the API's store:false setting; OpenAI's applicable API data retention policies still apply. Review your setup before submitting sensitive information. Generated research and drafts can be inaccurate and should be checked before use.

Service providers and retention

MongoDB Atlas stores account records, encrypted project and outreach records, and encrypted Gmail connection records. Our authenticated backend is hosted on Fly.io, sharing server infrastructure with another Ouro LLC app while using separate First Five accounts, database credentials and records. Public support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Google provides Gmail and authorization. OpenAI provides research and drafting. Delivery-status records are retained to prevent duplicate sends and removed when you delete your account. Sessions expire after 30 days. Daily usage counters expire after two days. Hosting providers may retain operational network and error logs under their policies. We do not sell your personal data, add advertising SDKs, or track you across other companies' apps.

Your choices and deletion

Settings lets you disconnect any Gmail account. Disconnecting removes that connection from First Five and attempts to revoke Google's token; existing project records and sent Gmail messages remain. Delete account removes your First Five server account, sessions, saved Gmail credentials, synced project and outreach records, and cached project records stored by the app on this device. Emails already sent remain in Gmail, under your control. Deleting the app removes its local project records according to your device's storage and backup behavior. Contact us for deletion assistance or privacy requests.

Security and children

Connections use HTTPS. We encrypt saved Google credentials and store the mobile session in the device's secure credential storage. Synced project records are encrypted in our database and cached in app-local device storage. No system can guarantee absolute security. First Five is a business outreach tool and is not intended for children.
